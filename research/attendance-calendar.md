**Design consequences, not inferred employment facts:** a calendar entry needs its date, local
time interval, applicable area, sector/entitlement condition, additional-versus-replacement
relationship and source version. A rostered holiday can be worked; a legal holiday alone does
not prove absence. An unresolved geographic match is not permission to apply a capital-city
calendar. School breaks, bank closures and local event days need their own classification.

Calendar compilation remains incomplete: uninspected historical/local sources, geographic
selection, special territories and later declarations require evidence before affected totals
can be called complete. No claim of a complete all-Australia holiday dataset is made.

## Proposed calculation inputs

Keep these distinct rather than asking for one universal number of working days:
- Selected financial year, work-base jurisdiction/locality and sourced holiday-calendar version.
- Regular or rotating roster, hours, breaks and remote/site assignment for each attendance period.
- Worked public holidays, applicable substitutions and part-day intervals.
- Leave entitlement, leave actually taken and paid/unpaid classification.
- Explicitly entered leave dates. The user rejected assuming entitlement is taken each year;
  no supplied leave dates means no entered leave adjustment, not proof of zero actual leave.
- Occasional site visits, travel between sites and other exceptions to the normal remote pattern.

The 52-week conversion is a user-approved standardisation for the paid-hours view. It is not
evidence of 52 weeks of attendance or a rule for counting dated pay events. Financial-year
attendance must use its own period and roster. Fully on-site work still has zero commute on
days with no attendance. A part-day absence does not automatically remove an entire trip.

The user selected an explicit per-offer choice of whether rostered public holidays are normally
worked, with dated exceptions. Neither choice is assumed for a new offer. Holiday applicability,
attendance, pay entitlement and penalty rates remain distinct inputs or verified calculations.

## Independent acceptance cases to prepare

These are observable requirements for future tests, not executed test results:
- A paid day off can preserve paid hours while contributing no worked or commute hours.
- An overlapping leave day and public holiday remove the same attendance interval once.
- A public holiday on a non-rostered day does not remove another workday automatically.
- A worked public holiday retains its actual work and site-travel quantities.
- A part-day holiday intersects the affected hours; it does not erase the whole rostered day.
- A remote day with an explicit site visit includes that visit, without creating duplicate trips.
- Calendar dates outside the selected financial year contribute nothing to that year's attendance.

Unresolved: complete local holiday data, non-NES arrangements, other absence categories,
roster/time-zone edge cases and independent numerical fixtures. Unknown calendar coverage
must be disclosed; it cannot be represented as no holidays.
