# Fresh tax, super and payroll defaults audit

Reviewed on 24 October 2026. Clock verification began at **2026-10-24 03:08:54 UTC**;
the final source review, including the kilometre-rate supplement, was at
**2026-10-24 03:43:54 UTC**.
Scope: current source behavior for 2025–26 and 2026–27 salary comparisons, including application
to someone living in South Australia. Earlier project research and documentation were not used as
evidence. Source reads establish current code behavior; they do not validate its calculations.
No application changes, tests, builds, calculators or personal-data submissions were made.

**Conclusion:** legal rates can be selected by financial year. Personal circumstances cannot be
inferred from a South Australian address. The current standard model supplies several circumstances
as explicit calculator conventions, including ordinary Medicare treatment, supplied-TFN payroll and
ordinary taxed-fund contributions. These are materially different from statutory defaults.

The principal risks are `ordinaryFund=yes` for every standard offer, `reportableSuperOffer` derived
solely from salary sacrifice despite other reportable contributions, and standard-deduction
eligibility derived from broad employment labels. These need a product decision or a narrower
derivation, not an unsupported assertion that the person satisfies the conditions.

## Gaps and proof limits

- This is a fresh defaults audit, not a revalidation of every stored tax coefficient. LITO endpoints,
  final assessment precision, HELP's 2026–27 indexed operational examples and every family exemption
  calculation were not freshly certified. Their presence in code is not proof.
- Exact reportable-contribution year treatment was indexed on an ATO source but its body could not
  be retrieved here. `reportableSuperSameYear=yes` remains a modelling convention in this audit.
- The freshly read ordinary Medicare statutory amounts apply currently to 2025–26 onward. They
  must not be presented as proof that no later law can change 2026–27 assessment thresholds.
- No fresh evidence makes every employee label an ordinary PAYG/SG case, or every SA fund a taxed
  accumulation fund. Bonus eligibility, salary super-inclusion and household facts remain separate.
- Mixed/GST-free supply classification, complete GST/PSI calculations, special funds, cap adjustments,
  other offsets and special study-loan schemes require their own fresh evidence before changing their
  behavior or claiming support.
- The kilometre-rate gap is closed for both selected years by F14–F16. This does not establish a
  particular journey's deductibility, actual vehicle expenditure or any rate after 2026–27.
- Source-body review does not prove browser behavior, code arithmetic, imported-data compatibility
  or test results. No execution claims are made.
