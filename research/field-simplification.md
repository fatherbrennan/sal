# Field simplification — 23 October 2026

The user requested an investigation of every field and removal of irrelevant questions. The
initial editor rendered all 60 offer fields and 32 personal-tax fields, including contractor,
family and technical declarations regardless of their relevance. The schema and editor shared
one field list, so deleting definitions would also reject existing IndexedDB records and backups.

The implementation separates the editor projection from validation. No stored field is stripped,
renamed or coerced. Schema version 1 and the IndexedDB store remain compatible. Existing records
without a standard-model selection retain their previous calculations. New records receive the
approved optional $0 amounts; loading or importing old records never adds those defaults.

## Bonus and commission super control

Approved on 24 October 2026. One choice describes the entered amount:
- **Cash before tax, plus super**: employer super is additional.
- **Cash before tax and super combined**: the quote contains cash and employer super.
- **Cash before tax, no super applies**: a confirmed exception, not a waiver or earnings-limit setting.

New ordinary bonus rows retain their approved super-eligible preset; the combined choice remains
unanswered until the quote basis is selected. Existing incomplete answers stay unchanged and their
known facts appear in the help text. Unrelated edits preserve those facts and independent results.
Choosing a treatment saves both stored answers together. Clearing it makes both unknown. A confirmed
cash-only choice stores an exclusive cash amount with no eligible earnings. Existing inclusive
cash-only records remain unchanged until explicitly edited. Schema version 2 and tax rules are unchanged.

## Standard estimate boundary

`estimateMode=standard` is a calculation convention, not evidence of the person's circumstances.
It uses ordinary full-year resident adult treatment; equal ordinary employee base-pay periods
with TFN-provided withholding scales; an ordinary eligible base for employer super; and the
bounded 15% fund-contribution model. Salary sacrifice is modelled in the selected reporting year
and evenly across representative periods. Annual bonuses are not presumed evenly spread for
quarterly SG limits. Exact final assessed-tax precision remains unavailable.

These conventions are disclosed in the form and result limitations. Existing values take
precedence, including No and blank. Derived values are ephemeral and recomputed after parent
edits; they are never written over user input. Personal household, hospital-cover, loan and
threshold choices remain explicit. Selecting original mode exposes independent declarations and
reporting overrides. Unsupported circumstances leave dependent results unavailable.

The dedicated ATO specialist checked current primary sources: [ordinary PAYG scales T22](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2026L00716/asmade/2026-06-12/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
[employee standard deduction, Act 49 Schedule 4 T07](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00049/asmade/2026-06-26/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
[HELP section 154-1 T12](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01234/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
and [reportable employer super T53](https://caat-p-001.sitecorecontenthub.cloud/api/public/content/0-7350b1a9-c870-4d9b-b4dc-1506974b918e), pages 3–7.
The sources establish the relevant distinctions; they do not establish a particular user's facts.
